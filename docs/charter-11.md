## 🧭 The Core Model and Turtle Soup

* **Timestamp:** [00:07:40]
  ![Frame 00:07:40](assets/charter-11/00-07-40.png)
* The relationship Mado wants burned into memory: the **1:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m. candles build the range**, and the **9:00 a.m. candle performs the manipulation and expansion** — he calls this the **core model**, the bread-and-butter sequence (1 AM range → 5 AM manipulation → 9 AM continuation/expansion) [00:07:40-00:08:09].
  ![Frame 00:07:54](assets/charter-11/00-07-54.png)
* **Turtle soup** is redefined simply as price breaking a short-term high or low, trapping the traders positioned on that breakout, then ripping in the opposite direction — a callback to the original turtle-trading breakout strategy that smart money is said to hunt against [00:08:36-00:08:58].
  ![Frame 00:08:45](assets/charter-11/00-08-45.png)
* The 9:00 a.m. candle needs that soup from either the 1:00 a.m. or the 5:00 a.m. range to be valid [00:09:04-00:09:15].
* Unlike other CRT models that Mado has given three profiles for, the 9:00 a.m. CRT comes down to **two clean paths: New York reversal and New York continuation** — and each of those two profiles actually has **two variations** [00:09:15-00:09:36].
  ![Frame 00:09:18](assets/charter-11/00-09-18.png)
* **Profile 1 — New York reversal**, one-sentence version: the 1:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m. candles do the range-building, and the 9:00 a.m. candle performs manipulation **and** reversal [00:09:40-00:09:57].
  ![Frame 00:09:40](assets/charter-11/00-09-40.png)

* **Version 1.2** is described as a little more layered: it brings the **9:00 p.m. range** into the picture, moving through the 1:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m. candles, which already do their own manipulation and expansion toward a higher-timeframe key level — and then the 9:00 a.m. candle takes it one step further with its **own** manipulation right into that same key level [00:11:05-00:11:55].

## 🔄 New York Continuation — Versions 1.1 and 1.2

* **Timestamp:** [00:12:49]
  ![Frame 00:12:49](assets/charter-11/00-12-49.png)
* **Profile 2 — New York continuation, version 1.1:** this is explicitly described as basically the **core model** again — the 1:00 a.m. candle ranges, the 5:00 a.m. candle manipulates, and the 9:00 a.m. candle continues in the same direction the manipulation was already pointing, without a sweep against the trend [00:13:20-00:14:05].
  ![Frame 00:13:20](assets/charter-11/00-13-20.png)
* **Version 1.2** has **four distinct phases instead of three**: the 9:00 p.m. candle ranges, the 1:00 a.m. candle manipulates and expands, the 5:00 a.m. candle re-ranges, and the 9:00 a.m. candle sweeps and continues [00:14:10-00:14:48].
  ![Frame 00:14:10](assets/charter-11/00-14-10.png)
* Mado notes this version becomes much easier to spot once a trader has watched enough charts — the pattern starts revealing itself before it even fully completes [00:14:33-00:15:06].
  ![Frame 00:14:33](assets/charter-11/00-14-33.png)
* Graphically: London comes in, manipulates the range, and continues higher — while price is consolidating it is also building short-term liquidity, which New York later uses as a continuation mechanism rather than reversing completely [00:15:06-00:16:20].
  ![Frame 00:15:06](assets/charter-11/00-15-06.png)
* The underlying logic: the low (or high) of the existing move becomes the short-term liquidity pool New York is likely to find, and context — **liquidity, location, timing, and existing order flow** together — determines whether a given tap of that pool should be read as continuation, not any single piece in isolation. Mado explicitly tells viewers to backtest this on their own charts rather than take the rule at face value [00:16:20-00:17:20].

## 🎯 The Draw on Liquidity Gatekeeper

* **Timestamp:** [00:17:20]
  ![Frame 00:17:20](assets/charter-11/00-17-20.png)
* You trade the **New York reversal** only when the **daily draw on liquidity has already been hit** and a reversal scenario is actually forming there — not "I think it might reverse." The daily DOL needs to be tagged first [00:17:20-00:17:58].
  ![Frame 00:17:20](assets/charter-11/00-17-20.png)
* Conversely, you trade the **New York continuation** only when the **daily draw on liquidity has not been reached yet** — meaning price hasn't already blown through the low or high of the day, and Asia/London did not already expand into it [00:17:58-00:18:21].
  ![Frame 00:17:58](assets/charter-11/00-17-58.png)
* Mado flags that both profiles start from the exact same early candle sequence — DOL status is what actually tells them apart, and he defers the full mechanics of identifying DOL to a later part of C3, calling it "not filler" but the piece that makes everything else connect [00:18:21-00:19:27].

## 🧮 Directional Bias and Candle Anatomy

* **Timestamp:** [00:19:34]
  ![Frame 00:19:34](assets/charter-11/00-19-34.png)
* The concise directional-bias rule: **buy below the 9:00 a.m. opening price, sell above it**. If price is trading below where the 9:00 a.m. candle opened, you're looking for buys; above it, you're looking for sells — the open is the dividing line [00:19:34-00:19:58].
  ![Frame 00:19:34](assets/charter-11/00-19-34.png)
* **Bullish sequence:** open → low → purge the 1:00 a.m. (or 5:00 a.m.) low at the key level → Model 1 confirmation → expansion. **Bearish sequence** is the mirror image: open → high → purge the 1:00 a.m. or 5:00 a.m. high at the key level → Model 1 → expansion to the downside [00:20:00-00:20:22].
  ![Frame 00:20:00](assets/charter-11/00-20-00.png)

## ⏱️ Timing and the M15 Entry Model

* **Timestamp:** [00:20:24]
  ![Frame 00:20:24](assets/charter-11/00-20-24.png)
* A setup without the right timing is called a coincidence waiting to fail. The **primary window** is roughly **9:00-10:00 a.m.**, with a **secondary window around 9:30-10:30 a.m.** for setups that develop slightly later — reading a setup inside these windows is given much higher-probability than one that fires once the market has already "cooled off" [00:20:24-00:21:07].
  ![Frame 00:20:24](assets/charter-11/00-20-24.png)
* Time is said to filter out a large percentage of false setups on its own.
* The execution timeframe is the **15-minute chart**, not the 1-minute or 5-minute, and the entry model is **Model 1**: the candle that dips below a prior low (or above a prior high) and then gets engulfed by the following candle or candles — described as the mechanical, repeatable footprint of smart money reclaiming that level [00:21:17-00:21:59].
  ![Frame 00:21:23](assets/charter-11/00-21-23.png)
