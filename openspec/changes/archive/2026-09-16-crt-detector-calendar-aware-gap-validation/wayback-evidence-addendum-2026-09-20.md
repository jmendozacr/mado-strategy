# Wayback Evidence Addendum — Historical Exness Pages

## Verdict

Wayback Machine captures provide dated, Exness-originated evidence for selected holiday notices and show that official instrument-hours pages were captured from 2024 onward. They do **not** provide complete, server-specific evidence for `EURUSDm`, `GBPUSDm`, and `DXYm` across the inclusive `2022-08-08` through `2026-08-19` policy window. B3–B6 therefore remain open, no calendar activation is authorized, and a written Exness support/compliance response is still necessary.

This addendum was produced on 2026-09-20, after the change was archived. It records later read-only research and does not revise, replace, or reopen the archived verdict in `design.md` or `evidence-report.md`.

Research boundary:

- No real market dataset was accessed or inspected.
- No existing archived artifact was modified.
- No Exness contact was made.
- No captured page was treated as proof beyond the claims visible in its retained historical version.
- The 2024–2026 page bodies were not extracted during this research; their capture timestamps and URLs are inventory evidence only.

## What the Research Established

1. Exness Help Center pages have retainable, timestamped Wayback captures.
2. Article ID `4405235684498` was reused: it carried holiday content in 2022–2023 and instrument-hours content from 2024 onward.
3. Selected historical holiday pages contain exact GMT+0 trading changes, but the extracted claims do not cover the target instruments and full policy window.
4. The requested current holiday article ID, `17923046759836`, returned no successful 200 capture in the bounded CDX query. A broader non-200 query timed out, so absence of every possible capture is not proven.

## Article-ID Reuse Warning

Article ID `4405235684498` is not a stable semantic identifier:

- 2022–2023 captures identify it as “Holiday market hours.”
- 2024 captures identify it as “What are the trading hours for all instruments.”
- Later captures identify it as “Instrument trading hours.”

Therefore:

- Do not infer that 2024 instrument tables existed in 2022–2023.
- Do not infer policy continuity from a shared Zendesk article ID.
- Bind every retained claim to the exact replay timestamp, original URL, page title, embedded update timestamp, and retrieved bytes.

- [ ] Query CDX for the exact official URL, capture timestamp, response status, digest, MIME type, and length.
- [ ] Fetch the exact `id_` replay rather than relying on a calendar dot, title, snippet, or timestamp pattern.
- [ ] Retain the response bytes and relevant archive response metadata.
- [ ] Record retrieval time and calculate a project-selected cryptographic hash, at minimum SHA-256.
- [ ] Extract the page title, canonical URL, embedded article update timestamp, instrument names, timezone, closure/reopen timestamps, and effective period.
- [ ] Distinguish a holiday name from an actual full closure or early-close claim.
- [ ] Map every claim to broker, server/account class, instrument, granularity, and the inclusive policy window.
- [ ] Reconcile duplicates, overlaps, conflicts, corrections, and article-ID reuse.
- [ ] Obtain the independent attestation required by the future activation design.
- [ ] Start a separate authorized SDD change before creating a calendar artifact, new lineage, or runtime authority.

Failure at any step leaves the corresponding gap unresolved. There is no fallback from rejected evidence to the legacy allowlist for an activated pair.

## Support Request Still Required

A written Exness support/compliance response is still required. It should explicitly provide or confirm:

- Broker and server scope: `Exness`, `Exness-MT5Trial11`, or the exact historical demo-server policy class that governed it.
- Instrument mapping: whether `EURUSDm`, `GBPUSDm`, and `DXYm` used the stated schedules throughout the window.
- Weekly sessions: exact UTC/GMT+0 open and close times by weekday for each target instrument.
- Seasonal policy: summer and winter UTC windows, transition rule, transition dates, and whether the server clock remained UTC+0.
- Holiday policy: every full closure, late open, early close, and reopen timestamp for each target instrument from `2022-08-08` through `2026-08-19` inclusive.
- DXYm classification: the underlying Exness instrument class and whether its hours differed from forex pairs.
- Effective periods: dates on which any schedule or classification changed.
- Historical applicability: confirmation that the response describes the policy actually in effect, not only the current published schedule.
- Retention permission/provenance: a dated written reply or attached historical records that can be retained and hashed.

Until those points are evidenced and a separate activation change is approved, the dormant contract and existing fail-closed behavior remain authoritative.
