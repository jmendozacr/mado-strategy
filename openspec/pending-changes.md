## Discovery — counterfactual measurement (2026-09-03, not tied to a single change)

- **[discovery, feeds the paused `crt-detector-key-level-confluence` program decision]** A one-off counterfactual (temp patch to `detector/backtest/simulate.py::_population_cuts`, reverted clean) let `key_level is None` signals through to simulation, to test charter-1 [00:12:02]'s claim that Key-Level-credited signals outperform non-credited ones — never measured before (prior runs only measured EXCLUSION %). Result, pooled across EURUSDm/GBPUSDm/DXYm: not-credited 27.27% winrate / +0.400 avg R (n=242) vs FVG-credited 26.63%/+0.343R (n=7410) vs OrderBlock-credited 23.33%/+0.528R (n=90) — not-credited performs AT LEAST as well as credited. Per-instrument results actively DISAGREE (EURUSDm/DXYm favor not-credited; GBPUSDm inverts hard, 8.70% winrate n=46) — the disagreement itself is informative: no consistent signal that confluence-gate credit predicts outcome. Sample sizes for not-credited (46-120/instrument) are small. Engram `project/pending-changes` (obs #303, discovery section).
- **[discovery, follow-up test — the mechanism refinement did NOT rescue the signal]** A manual visual review found `_key_level_credit_map`/`_purge_wick_extremes` can credit a Key Level off a weak, incidental wick (5 pips) on a candle whose real dominant move was 121 pips the OTHER way (dual-sided-breach candles, ~14% of real H4 purges). Tested the fix: restrict crediting to only the dominant-magnitude side on dual-sided breaches, re-ran all 3 instruments (1641/3394 dual-sided purges flipped which extreme got checked). Result: not-credited 28.12%/+0.4065R vs fvg 26.58%/+0.3440R vs ict_order_block 24.14%/+0.4035R — statistically flat vs the unrefined baseline above, same ordering, no gap opened. Reverted clean. Recommendation: do NOT proceed to `crt-detector-breaker-block` (#3) under the current confluence-gate operationalization — it would likely reproduce the same restrictive/no-edge result a 3rd time; reconsider the confluence-gate concept itself before investing further sub-changes. Engram `project/pending-changes` (obs #303, discovery section).
