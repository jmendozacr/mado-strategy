## Documented conventions (engineering assumptions, not charter rules)

- **H4** candles are NY-feeder-anchored: opens at 1:00/5:00/9:00/13:00/17:00/21:00 America/New_York, resolved through `America/New_York` with full EST/EDT handling (`feeder_open_utc`). This mirrors the strategy's own session structure.
- **Daily** candles open at **17:00 America/New_York** (`bucket_close_utc`/`DAILY_HOURS`). No charter defines a Daily boundary; this was chosen and confirmed with Josué specifically to avoid reintroducing the broker-H4-vs-feeder-H4 discrepancy that originally motivated this whole audit. It is a visual/context convention only — it feeds no trading rule, filter, or validation.
- **Native broker Daily (`data/*_D1_*.csv`) anchors to 00:00 UTC**, confirmed by direct price matching (not inferred): the native file's OPEN for calendar date `D` equals the M15 bar's OPEN at `D 00:00:00 UTC`, verified on 2016-03-10 and 2016-03-14, straddling the March 2016 US DST change with no shift. The two Daily series are therefore genuinely different conventions, not the same data differently labeled — any comparison against D3/C2 or the detector in Stage 2 must account for this, not treat it as a bug in either side.
- Both H4 and Daily, and the extended historical context pages, never fabricate or interpolate a missing M15 candle; a real gap before the cursor is reported honestly (`integrity: "gap"`), and the standard Friday 17:00-Sunday 17:00 NY forex closure is never counted against completeness.

## Known limitations (not fixed, out of scope for Stage 1)

- `historical_context()` performs a full linear scan of the relevant M15 CSV on every page request (no index). On the real multi-year dataset this is ~0.8-0.9s per page — correct, but paginating back many months in the live UI is noticeably slow. Harmless for the 30-case pilot's expected usage (a handful of pages per session).
- Stage 2 (automated human-vs-D3/C2 comparison) and discrepancy classification are not implemented.

## What this checkpoint guarantees

Any decision or annotation recorded from this commit onward carries, already stamped by the server: the exact `dataset_version` (per-pair M15 file hash above) and `rules_version` (charter+causal-code hash above) in effect when it was saved, plus the replay cursor and wall-clock recording time. Reproducing the exact conditions behind any pilot decision means checking out this commit (or a later one, diffed against it) and reading those two stamped fields back from `runtime/events.jsonl`.
